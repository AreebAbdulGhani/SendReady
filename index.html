01 / The Problem

Express exports images and PDFs. It does not export email HTML.

A small business owner designs a beautiful newsletter in Adobe Express. Brand colors match. Typography is clean. Then comes the moment of truth: sending it. And the whole workflow falls apart.

Marketers screenshot the design and paste it as a giant broken image into Mailchimp. They rebuild from scratch in a clunky email editor. Or they pay a developer to hand-code HTML. Each option kills the speed advantage of using Express in the first place.

02 / The Product

A focused, single-purpose add-on. One job, done right.

MJML-backed output

Compiles to MJML — the open-source email framework used by Airbnb. Handles every Outlook bug, every mobile reflow edge case. Bulletproof as hand-coded email HTML.

Live multi-client preview

See exactly how the email renders in Gmail, Outlook 365, Apple Mail, and mobile — before you copy the HTML. Unsafe elements flagged with one-click fallbacks.

Copy-paste universal compatibility

Pure HTML that pastes into Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot, ConvertKit, MailerLite, Constant Contact. No API integration. No platform lock-in.

Minimal AI, maximum reliability

AI plays a small, tasteful role: subject-line variants and alt-text for accessibility. The core conversion logic is deterministic MJML compilation, not AI.

03 / How It Works

Three screens. One minute. Every major email client covered.

01

Design in Express

Use templates, brand kit, fonts, stock images — the way you already design everything else. SendReady doesn't add friction to the design phase.

02

Open SendReady

The panel reads the document through the Document Sandbox API, maps every layer to its email-safe MJML equivalent, and compiles to production-grade HTML. Under two seconds.

03

Preview across clients

A split-screen live preview shows exactly how the email renders in Gmail, Outlook 365, Apple Mail, and mobile webkit. Unsafe elements get flagged with a one-click fallback.

04

Export

Copy HTML drops paste-ready output onto the clipboard. Download HTML saves a file for developers. AI layer offers three subject-line variants plus auto-generated alt-text.

05

Paste and send

Paste into Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot, or any email platform that accepts HTML. Finished Express design to inbox takes minutes instead of hours.
